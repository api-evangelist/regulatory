FINRA Essentials: Your Guide to Key Regulatory Touchpoints K31999 Tue, 05/26/2026 - 13:29 This video series guides member firms through key interactions with FINRA, transforming regulatory touchpoints into clear, navigable processes. Each video features FINRA subject matter experts who provide insider perspectives, actionable guidance and valuable resources to help firms engage more effectively with FINRA. Whether you're preparing for an examination, considering how to share cybersecurity incident information or looking to participate in the rulemaking process, this series offers straightforward explanations and practical guidance designed to enhance your regulatory experience. By increasing transparency and highlighting available resources, "FINRA Essentials" empowers member firms to approach regulatory interactions with confidence and clarity. Meet the FINRA's Office of the Ombuds Featuring Sarah Gill, FINRA Ombuds. When firms or their associated persons need a confidential, impartial place to raise concerns about FINRA, the Office of the Ombuds is that resource. In this episode, Sarah explains what the office can — and can't — do, and the kinds of issues it's designed to help with. Resources FINRA's Office of the Ombuds FINRA HelpLine Inside the Rulemaking Process Every FINRA rule starts with an idea — and a long process before it reaches your firm. This episode follows a rule from concept through advisory committee review, public comment, FINRA Board approval and SEC review, and shows where industry voices shape the outcome along the way.
